# SYNC AI 🚀
### AI-Powered Smart Hackathon Team Matching Platform

**Right Student. Right Team. Right Hackathon.**

SYNC AI is a smart platform designed to help students find the right teammates for hackathons based on their skills, interests, strengths, and project requirements. It also helps students discover hackathon opportunities and build balanced teams for collaborative innovation.

## 🌟 Problem Statement

Students often struggle to find suitable teammates for hackathons. They may not know students with complementary skills, have limited networking opportunities, or find it difficult to identify the right hackathons for their interests.

SYNC AI aims to simplify this process by bringing hackathon discovery and team matching together in one platform.

## 💡 Our Solution

SYNC AI focuses on helping students connect with compatible teammates instead of relying only on friends or random team selection.

The platform is designed to support skill-based matching, hackathon discovery, team collaboration, and continuous student growth.

## ✨ Key Features

- **AI-Powered Team Matching** – Identify potential teammates based on skills, interests, and team requirements.
- **Hackathon Discovery** – Explore hackathon opportunities and find events relevant to your interests.
- **Student Profiles** – Showcase skills, interests, achievements, and learning progress.
- **Skill Assessment** – Evaluate relevant skills to support more informed team matching.
- **Team Collaboration** – Support communication and coordination among accepted team members.
- **AI Recommendations** – Receive suggestions for potential teammates and relevant opportunities.
- **Growth Tracking** – Encourage continuous learning and skill improvement.
- **Team Formation** – Allow students to express interest in joining teams, with team leaders reviewing requests.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Simplify the process of finding hackathon teammates.
- Encourage collaboration between students with complementary skills.
- Improve access to hackathon opportunities.
- Support beginners and students with different skill levels.
- Promote continuous learning, teamwork, and innovation.

## 🔄 How It Works

1. Register and create a student profile.
2. Complete the required verification process.
3. Add skills, interests, and preferred project domains.
4. Complete relevant skill assessments, where implemented.
5. Discover suitable hackathons.
6. Explore potential teammates recommended by the matching system.
7. Express interest in joining a team.
8. The team leader reviews the request.
9. Accepted members collaborate on the project.

## 🚀 What Makes SYNC AI Different?

SYNC AI focuses on **finding the right combination of people**, not simply selecting students with the highest scores.

The matching approach aims to consider complementary skills, shared interests, project requirements, and opportunities for students to learn from one another.
